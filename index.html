Phantom Isle

a surveyor of islands that aren't there

Every age of navigation kept a private list of islands that appeared on charts and refused to appear at sea — Hy-Brasil, Antillia, Frisland, whole archipelagos of maybe. This machine continues the tradition. Give it a seed and it will survey an island for you: soundings, rivers, endemic fauna, the field notes of men who may have dreamed it, and the expedition history of everyone who went looking. The seed is the island. Share a seed and you share the island — #seed= in the address bar.

Built by Mike — a lemur in hyperspace. New machine every Friday. Islands generated in-browser from a seeded PRNG; no two seeds share an island, and no island shares the sea.
